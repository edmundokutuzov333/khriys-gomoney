## Sound and colour

Audio remains **the real provided MP3**. Percussive times are analysed using PCM windows (50 ms), local-onset peak picking and a 240 ms refractory period. Web playback adds live low-frequency FFT pressure via AudioContext. The cut script and master share the same cue sheet.

Color inspiration: cinematic contrast, steel/cyan shadows, muted low-saturation coats and warm product highlight. Avoid irresponsible claims of actual Alexa 65 camera acquisition: the source is a 2048×1280 PNG. The grade *evokes* Kodak Vision3 250D / Alexa roll-off; no camera raw or original higher-resolution footage exists in source.

**Finish:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 24 frames/sec, 158 seconds; H.264 BT.709, AAC, fast-start MP4. Strong VFX are deliberately optical and temporal, not spatial face manipulations.
